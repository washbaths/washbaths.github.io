Ginny Void has been on their mat for nearly 20 years and teaching on and off for five. A 200-hour RYT and Reiki Level III practitioner, more and more they have been called to stillness — the discomfort, and necessity, of it. Their classes tune into the body's slower currents, weaving asana, breathwork, lymphatic work, and meditation into one integrative experience.

VVITCH VVINDOVV is the latest project from Cedar Cowart — the man behind Black Mica, Florida Ghost, and the proprietor of Crystal Cave Cassettes. Relying exclusively on synthesizers and ambient textures stretched, layered, looped, and processed across an array of outboard hardware, VVITCH VVINDOVV strips away the traditional instrumentation present in his other projects and focuses solely on how circuitry can make us feel. While Florida Ghost often feels like a warm blanket wrapping around the heart, VVITCH VVINDOVV feels like an unflinching stare into the void.
