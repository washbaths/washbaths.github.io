GINNY VOID

Ginny Void has been on their mat for nearly 20 years and teaching on and off for five. A 200-hour RYT and Reiki Level II practitioner, more and more they have been called to stillness — the discomfort, and necessity, of it. Their classes tune into the body's slower currents, weaving asana, breathwork, lymphatic work, and meditation into one integrative experience.


BRI BOWMAN

Bri Bowman is a sound and performance artist and herbalist exploring memory, loss, landscape, and belonging. Their work interweaves listening, collective composition, and sonic/sensory experimentation. They are currently focused on fresh water springs and ecological grief and perform as Water Through Stone.
